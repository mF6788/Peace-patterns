Welcome to Peace Patterns

Peace Patterns is a space for exploring the patterns that connect us: nature, human experience, creativity, relationships and the search for meaning.

Through photography, observations and reflections, this project looks for moments of stillness, connection and understanding in an often complicated world.

Each image tells a story. Each pattern offers an opportunity to notice something we might otherwise overlook.

This is an evolving collection of ideas, images and discoveries.
